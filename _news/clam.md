New preprint: "Instant Weight-Space Consolidation for Long-Term Memory in LLMs" is on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20759). CLAM compiles a stream of sessions into a LoRA adapter in one forward pass, with no gradient steps and no generation calls, so an agent's memory lives in its weights rather than in retrieved text.
